## Hat models — VoxelBear (CC BY)

Converted from the **Minecraft Hats Mini-pack 1–5** series by **VoxelBear** on Sketchfab
(<https://sketchfab.com/VoxelBear/models>), made in Blockbench. Licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)**.

**Attribution is required.** Any distribution of GlowberryClient that includes these files must
credit VoxelBear. The licence shown on the Mini-pack 3 page is CC BY; the other mini-packs are by
the same author.

Changes made during conversion: glTF → Bedrock `.geo.json` (per-face UVs kept), split into one file
per hat, and re-aimed so the front faces −Z and the hat sits on a head whose top is at y=32.
Textures are unchanged.
